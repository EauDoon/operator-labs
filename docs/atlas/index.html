Where did the event go?

      
Follow one fictional collector change through two checks. The candidate retains the resource and span fields. Its event population falls from one to zero.

      
Field counts · regression (exit 1)
TC004 flags the missing event field telemetry.event.class. TC005 records that fewer events carry the required field than in the baseline.

      
Coverage rates · unresolved (exit 2)
The baseline has one of one events with the field. The candidate has zero of zero. A rate delta cannot be calculated for an empty population. TC900 records that limitation.
